### What this screen should support (without bloating it)

* **Preference view** (attempt share by zone) — shown above
* Optional toggle: **Efficiency view** (FG% by zone) once you have enough data
* Optional toggle: **Recent form** (last 5 featured games shot diet)

### Important design rule

Show **attempt preference** by default, not accuracy.
Accuracy comes later, otherwise users misread small samples.
