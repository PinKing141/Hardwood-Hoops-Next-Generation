Sim Career Schedule (Final, Hands-Off Play-by-Play)
Playable = watched, decision-adjacent, high-impact simulations; no key-moment steering; no manual takeovers. Agency comes only from pre-game intent (tendencies/role/minutes) and post-game consequences.

Totals
- 24-30 featured play-by-play games per career stage span (hard cap).
- Featured = full play-by-play view, scout evaluation, media blurb, rankings recalc, recruiting interest update.
- Background sim = stat update and light perception nudge; no big reputation swings.

Junior Year — High School
- Featured games: 8-10 total.
- Structure: 6-7 regular-season featured; 2-3 playoff featured (single elimination). All other games background-simmed.
- Pre-game agency: usage emphasis (aggressive/balanced/low), shot profile bias (rim/mid/three), defensive focus (contain/disrupt), minutes target (light/normal/heavy).
- Purpose: establish statistical identity, create first scouting profile, determine AAU invitation tier. Identity over win count.

AAU Circuit (Between Junior and Senior)
- Featured games: 6-8 total across 3-4 events.
- Each event: 2 featured games (watched play-by-play); remaining games background-simmed.
- Each featured AAU game tagged as high-scout-presence, ranking-sensitive, media-visible.
- Pre-game agency: role expectation (primary/secondary/glue), risk appetite (safe/assertive), defensive assignment priority. No in-game control.
- Purpose: national ranking movement, scholarship tier changes, reputation swings (riser/plateau/faller). Perception overtakes stats.

Senior Year — High School
- Featured games: 10-12 total.
- Structure: 7-8 regular-season featured; 3-4 playoff featured (single elimination). Higher featured ratio than junior year.
- Weighting: senior featured > junior; playoff featured > regular; bad games hurt more; good games cement status rather than spike it.
- Purpose: lock star rating, lock scholarship tier, finalize recruiting narrative. Confirmation over discovery.

Total Breakdown (Locked)
- Junior HS: 8-10 featured play-by-play sims.
- AAU: 6-8 featured play-by-play sims.
- Senior HS: 10-12 featured play-by-play sims.
- Total: 24-30 featured play-by-play sims.

Simming Quality-of-Life
- Allow simming through games with interrupts on nationally televised games so the user can stop and watch.

Why This Fits a Sim Engine
- No reliance on player skill/input; no fake key moments; outcomes feel earned via tendencies/context.
- Watching stays meaningful instead of passive; careers stay tight (not 60+ watched games).

Hard Rules
- No full HS schedules fully watched; no AAU grind; no win-or-fail gates; no forced championships for progression. Trajectory over rings.

Immersion (Zero Gameplay Control)
- Pre-game scouting notes; post-game coach quotes; recruiting messages reacting to recent sims; media narratives shifting tone. Flavor only, never overrides the sim.
